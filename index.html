About me

Hi, I'm Tracy, you might know me as Kata.

I'm a Canadian creator with Dominican roots, and my content lives between two cultures and two languages. My audience is a real mix of English and Spanish speakers who show up for the same thing: honest, relatable content they can trust, not a hard sell.

I started out unboxing and reviewing PR packages on TikTok, and it has grown into full UGC: product reviews, unboxings and everyday lifestyle content that feels like a recommendation from a friend, not an ad.

Whether it's skincare, food, fashion, fitness or travel, I keep it natural and creative, so brands can show their products to real people in a way that builds trust and sparks enough interest to act.
